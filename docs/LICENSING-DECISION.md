# Licensing decision

The maintainer chose MIT and confirmed ownership of the original SK Protect scripts, tests, documentation, and artwork. Public reuse is allowed while the copyright and licence notice remain attached. `NOTICE.md` records the requested project and maintainer attribution.

## Option A: MIT

Short, familiar, permissive, and easy for contributors and directories to understand. It includes warranty and liability disclaimers. It does not include an express patent grant.

## Option B: Apache License 2.0

Permissive, but longer. It includes an express patent licence and patent-termination language, and requires preservation of notices and disclosure of modified files. This can be useful for a technically complex patch project, though it adds contributor/compliance detail.

The MIT licence covers only the maintainer's original patch scripts, tests, documentation, and owned artwork/contributions. It cannot grant rights to SKlauncher's proprietary executable, source, trademarks, or assets; those are not distributed here. `LICENSE` and `NOTICE.md` state that boundary clearly.
